What this ledger holds

Thirty-one projects spanning three years of coursework and independent work: predictive modeling on real and open datasets, business and strategy analytics for products and companies, database and network design, and system-level architecture.

Most were built with a team of two to ten people, under deadline, on a shared dataset none of us had seen before the brief. A few — the bankruptcy prediction capstone in particular — were solo, supervised work carried across two full project cycles.

I've also applied this outside the classroom as a Digital Planner Intern at Omnicom Media Group, running analysis on live client campaigns, and as an e-commerce seller managing my own storefronts on Tokopedia and Shopee.
